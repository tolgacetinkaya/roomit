# Roomit 0.4.4

- Scan data for one AI app directly from its card with **Scan app data**.
- Choose a single app or **All AI tools** in AI scan settings.
- See the app's measured total and open its scanned data in the storage map.
- Saved results retain the selected app; **Rescan** uses the same scope after restarting Roomit.
- Existing progress, cancellation, exclusions and stale-result warnings continue to apply. Failed scans preserve the previous result; completed or cancelled scans replace the latest saved snapshot.

This scan covers known per-user data locations. Custom model directories and project-local data outside those roots require a folder scan. Sizes do not establish whether data is unused. No deletion actions are included.

From 0.4.2 or later, choose **Roomit → Check for updates → Review update…**, then **Install and restart** or **Download latest portable EXE**. Installing an update from a portable copy installs Roomit; it does not overwrite the original portable EXE.
